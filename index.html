Serving Lahore Since 1984

Generations of Timber & Woodworking Expertise

At HSA & SONS, timber is our lifelong craft. Established 40+ years ago in the famous timber hub of Ravi Road, Lahore, we have earned the trust of homeowners, architects, and contractors as a premier source for raw hardwoods and bespoke doors.

We stock and season raw logs of Sagwan (Teak), European Ash wood, Royal Mahogany, and Diyar. Every plank undergoes strict moisture inspection so that doors, chokhats, and furniture frames never bend or crack under Lahore's climate extremes.
