Generations of Wood Craftsmanship

At HSA & SONS, wood is not just our business — it is our family tradition. Over the past four decades, we have evolved from a traditional timber yard at Ravi Road into Lahore's premier multi-branch destination for luxury solid wood entrance doors, contemporary interior flush doors, and modern PVC doors.

Every piece of wood that enters our workshop undergoes thorough inspection, moisture-level checks, and seasoning so your doors stand firm against humidity, dry winters, and everyday use.
